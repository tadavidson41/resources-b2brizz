Campaign performance0

LinkedIn campaigns that influenced pipe or revenue in-period. Pipe = Fibbler open + closed-lost; revenue = closed-won. 10 per page (or Show all). Expand a row for top 10 companies by revenue, then pipe. Names cleaned; type and Remarketing/Awareness badges replace raw format.

30-day rule: spend, impressions and clicks are September delivery. Pipe and deals credit a campaign only for qualified September deals it touched (Fibbler campaign-by-deal) when the campaign delivered impressions (LinkedIn daily data) inside the 30 days before deal creation. Campaigns with no September delivery that meet that test are listed with $0 September spend. The Pipeline total is unique open pipeline across credited deals, not Fibbler weighted pipeline.

Things to Know

  • Cohort. New-business deals created in September 2026 with 3+ LinkedIn paid clicks at the company in the 30 days before deal creation. Expansion held out.
  • Create vs close. Pipeline, deal table, journeys, and campaign-by-deal use create date. Influenced revenue uses close date (Closed Won only).
  • Spend. Overview spend is Fibbler account LinkedIn spend for September 2026 ($15,717). LinkedIn Campaign Manager reports $20,777 for September; Fibbler cost data lags for late-September campaigns. Efficiency tables use Fibbler campaign spend.
  • Pipeline vs revenue. Pipeline = open stages only. Revenue = Closed Won closed in-period.
  • Full month. September 2026 is a complete month (data as of Oct 2, 2026 ET).
  • Journeys. Cards show 12 months of paid activity ending on create, for every qualifying September company. Est. Spend uses 2026 non-Spotlight CPM/CPC (not booked spend). Spotlight excluded from the blend.
  • 30-day rule. September deals need 3+ paid clicks in the 30 days before deal creation. Fibbler has no lookback setting and reports clicks by month, so the 30-day count is a monthly-prorated estimate. Campaign credit requires the campaign to have touched the deal and to have delivered impressions (LinkedIn daily data) within the 30 days before creation.
  • Campaign performance. Mix and efficiency are campaign-level; pipe/rev can double-count across campaigns. Spotlight is out of ad-type charts. Expand a row for top companies on that campaign.
