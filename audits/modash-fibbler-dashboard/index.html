Things to Know

  • Influence. A deal counts when paid LinkedIn activity before the deal reaches the selected threshold: 3+ paid clicks, 3+ paid engagements, or 50+ paid impressions. Every deal number on the page uses the same filters.
  • Business type. New business and Existing business are the deal type. A missing, blank, or "-" deal type counts as Existing business. A deal name containing "upsell" or "expansion" (any case) counts as Existing business even when the type says New business. A hyphen in a company name is not a missing type. All includes both types, and each deal is listed once.
  • Create vs close. Pipeline, the deal table, campaign-by-deal cards, and journeys use the create date. Influenced revenue uses the close date (Closed Won only). A deal created last year and won this year counts as revenue in the period it closed.
  • Pipeline. Pipeline created includes every filtered deal created in the period, any stage. Open pipeline is deals that are not Closed Won or Closed Lost.
  • Currency. Ad spend is already in EUR and is not converted. Deal amounts are stored in USD and shown in EUR.
  • Time frames. YTD is Jan 1 to Nov 5, 2026. L3M is Aug 1 to Oct 31, 2026. LFM is October 2026. MTD is Nov 1 to Nov 5, 2026. November covers 5 of 30 days.
  • Coverage. Nov 5, 2026 snapshot for Fibbler Modash (cm3vweh7q008h8qqtv3e112i1) and LinkedIn ads account 510440948. All 58 campaigns in the extract are included. Spend sums those whole-euro rows (€164,662), which is €27 under Fibbler's same-day account total of €164,689. This file has 518 deals; Fibbler counted 519 influenced deals for Jan through Nov. Ten older campaigns appear on deal touchpoints and have no 2026 spend row. 79 deals have no campaign split.
  • Ad type mix. Spotlight ads are excluded. The account ran image and video ads in 2026.
