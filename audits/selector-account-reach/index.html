What changed

Reach +94 accounts, engaged doubledReached $5B+ prospects went 745 → 839 (60% → 64.7%) and engaged 18 → 37. 457 accounts (35%) still have zero paid impressions — the main gap to the 100% goal.
Cold CTR holding, CPC upCold Images CTR is flat (0.41% → 0.42%) but CPC rose $23.82 → $27.00. Remarketing CTR slipped 0.46% → 0.42% and CPC rose $26.97 → $36.76 as the small warm pool (2,256 members) saw higher frequency.
Stats 1/2 now lead; early winners fadedIn Cold 08.26 Images, Stats_1 and Stats_2 lead at 0.51% CTR (Stats_1 hits 0.69% in Big 10); in Remarketing, Q&A_2 (reduce MTTR) leads at 0.59%. Sep 17's top ads regressed: Social proof 2 0.59% → 0.46%, Bullet Points 2 0.57% → 0.48%. Time_Back_2 (0.24%) and Q&A 1/3 in Remarketing (0.07–0.21%) are the weakest — candidates to pause.
Mega-account concentration easingMicrosoft is still #1 but went from 47% of top-20 impressions on Sep 17 (10,008 of 21,454) to 22% now (8,299 of 36,896); Meta, Oracle, Capital One and Google now carry more of the top. New entrants (DoorDash, Goldman Sachs, AMD, Mastercard, Expedia) show the spread widening beyond the original 10 mega accounts.
Engagement objective: more clicks, fewer site visitsIn Cold Engagement 09.26, Time_Back_1 (1.34% CTR) and Product_Marketing_2 (0.83%) get the highest click rates, but less than half are landing-page clicks (0.46% / 0.38% LP CTR) — similar to Website Visits on site traffic, at a lower CPC.

Methodology & gaps

  • Account list: Target_Accounts_5B_Plus sheet (1,341 rows) minus 45 current customers (HubSpot export, Sep 17) by exact normalized name = 1,296. Sep 17 base was 1,242.
  • Reach/engagement: Fibbler company engagement, rolling last 60 days, Cold Audience campaigns. LinkedIn only reports companies with 3+ impressions.
  • Top 20: Fibbler “Cold Audience” + “Warm Audience” campaigns (871631906, 886742336, 904204446, 904339926, 904448636, 875351356) minus Spotlight (871872106); Boost Post (904872626) and the Jul 15 Website conversions campaigns are not included. Per company, $5B+ prospects only. Eng. = paid engagements, Clicks = paid clicks; “–” = 0.
  • Campaign/theme/ad metrics: LinkedIn Campaign Manager analytics (ad account 508638915), Aug 26 – Oct 2, 2026. Sep 17 window not stated in the doc (assumed launch – ~Sep 16).
  • Gaps: ad thumbnails not available through the connector; the Sep 17 top-20 cut couldn’t be reproduced exactly, so impression deltas are directional.
