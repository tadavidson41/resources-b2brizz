Landing page change: conversion rate before vs after

Jellyfish Google Ads. New paid landing pages and new ad copy went live Oct 14, 2026. Before = Oct 2 to Oct 13 (12 days). After = Oct 15 to Nov 3 (20 days, updated Nov 3). Conversions = GA4 Demo Request Submission + GA4 Email Form Submission, for both brand and non-brand. Thank you page views are left out because they usually fire right after a demo submission, which would count the same person twice.
