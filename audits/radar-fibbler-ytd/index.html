Things to Know

  • Cohort. New business only: Salesforce deal type Initial Sale with 3+ LinkedIn paid clicks at the company (Fibbler, 12-month click lookback). Time Frame switches the window; rules stay the same. Month to date is the default view; add ?period=ytd, l3m, lfm or mtd to the link to open a specific view.
  • Create vs close. Pipeline, the deal table and journeys use create date. Influenced revenue uses close date (Closed Won only), so a deal won in the period is listed even if it was created earlier (JetBlue and Domino’s were created in 2025 and closed won in 2026).
  • Spend. Overview spend is LinkedIn account 503449730 spend in-window (Oct is MTD). Efficiency tables use Fibbler campaign spend, which can differ by a few dollars. YTD account spend $114,718.
  • Pipeline vs revenue. Open pipeline = any stage that is not Closed Won or Closed Lost. Revenue = Closed Won closed in-period. 13 of 22 open cohort deals have no amount in Salesforce, so pipeline is a floor (most October deals have no amount yet).
  • Not counted. Deals under 3 paid clicks are left out even when Fibbler shows them as influenced (for example QuikTrip, $167,400 Closed Won Oct 14, and Cox Automotive, $200,000, both 0 paid clicks), so Fibbler’s own totals run higher than this page.
  • Partial month. October 2026 is partial (Fibbler data as of Oct 28 ET; 28 of 31 days). Last 3 months = Jul to Sep 2026; last full month = September 2026.
  • Reveal Accounts switch. Reveal Accounts is turned off until Tim sends the Radar Reveal target account list. It is wired to data.targetAccountFilter, so adding the list and rebuilding turns it on. When on, it recomputes overview KPIs, charts, the deal table, campaign-by-deal cards, journeys, and campaign buckets. Fibbler campaign-level totals in the campaign list, summary, and efficiency cannot be split by account and stay the same.
  • Journeys. Cards show 12 months of paid activity ending on create. Fibbler journey data was pulled for Nov 2025 to Oct 2026, so earlier months show as zero for deals created early in 2026. Est. Spend uses Aug+Sep 2026 account-level CPM/CPC (not booked spend).
  • Campaign buckets. Radar Reveal, Reveal Webinars, Remarketing, and Protect roll up campaigns by name. Spend, impressions, and clicks cover every bucket campaign in the period. Pipeline and revenue count each cohort deal once per bucket; a deal touched by two buckets shows in both.
  • Campaign performance. Segments: Radar Reveal, Reveal Webinars, Remarketing, Protect, Industry segments (QSR, Travel, Airlines, Financial/Payments, Marketplace, Retail, WFM/Logistics), Other. Mix and efficiency are campaign-level; pipe/rev can double-count across campaigns. Spotlight is out of ad-type charts. Company drilldowns list cohort deals the campaign touched.
