Things to Know

  • Cohort. New Business (Inbound/Outbound), Cross-sell, and Upsell with 3+ LinkedIn paid clicks at the company. Renewals are excluded from main KPIs. Time Frame switches the window; rules stay the same.
  • Create vs close. Pipeline, deal table, journeys, and campaign-by-deal use create date. Influenced revenue uses close date (Closed Won only).
  • Spend. Overview spend is account LinkedIn spend in-window (Oct is MTD). Efficiency tables use Fibbler campaign spend. YTD account spend $270,016.
  • Pipeline vs revenue. Open pipeline = any stage that is not Closed Won or Closed Lost (Demo highlighted yellow). Revenue = Closed Won closed in-period.
  • Partial month. October 2026 is partial (Fibbler data as of Oct 7 ET; 7 of 31 days). Last 3 months = Jul to Sep 2026 full months; last full month = September 2026.
  • Partnership. Partnership accounts come from the Chili Piper HubSpot partnership company list (605 companies). 55 of 212 influenced companies (86 deals) match by company name after normalizing case, punctuation, and legal suffixes. They carry a Partnership tag, and the Without partnership deals overview removes them. Ad spend is the same in both views.
  • Journeys. When present, cards show 12 months of paid activity ending on create. Est. Spend uses Aug+Sep 2026 blended CPM/CPC (not booked spend).
  • Campaign buckets. 1-to-1 ABM, Mid-Market, and Strategic roll up campaigns by name. Spend, impressions, and clicks cover every bucket campaign in the period. Pipeline and revenue count each cohort deal once per bucket (a deal touched by several 1 to 1 campaigns counts once); a deal touched by two buckets shows in both. Deals without Fibbler campaign touchpoints cannot be bucketed, so bucket pipeline and revenue are floors.
  • Campaign performance. Segments: Remarketing, Mid-Market, Strategic (incl. 1 to 1 / Enterprise). Mix and efficiency are campaign-level; pipe/rev can double-count across campaigns. Spotlight is out of ad-type charts.
  • Ambiguous campaigns. Names that do not clearly match a segment are listed for Tim in chili-piper-fibbler-dashboard-ambiguous-campaigns.md.
