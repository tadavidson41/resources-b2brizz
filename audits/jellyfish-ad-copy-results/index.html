Top-performing ad in each ad group

Each preview combines the ad group's highest-CTR pinned headline 1, its two highest-CTR rotating headlines and its two highest-CTR descriptions. Only assets with at least 100 impressions are included.

How to read this

  • Google Ads does not report clicks for each full headline and description combination, so these previews are built from each asset's own CTR.
  • A click counts toward every asset shown in that ad, so asset CTRs point to what works but cannot be fully separated.
  • Headlines marked "keyword insertion" swap in the searcher's keyword; the text shown is the default.
  • Cursor and Copilot Impact are left out of the previews because no asset reached 100 impressions yet.
