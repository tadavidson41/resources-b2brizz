How to read this

  • Google Ads does not report clicks for each full headline and description combination, so these previews are built from each asset's own CTR.
  • A click counts toward every asset shown in that ad, so asset CTRs point to what works but cannot be fully separated.
  • Only headlines and descriptions with at least 100 impressions are ranked, so a low CTR reflects real volume rather than a handful of impressions.
  • Headlines marked "keyword insertion" swap in the searcher's keyword; the text shown is the default.
  • Sitelinks are the account's own, chosen by how often each one served with that ad group from October 15 to 27. Google shows anywhere from zero to four, so the previews vary.
  • Cursor and Copilot Impact are left out because no asset reached 100 impressions yet.
