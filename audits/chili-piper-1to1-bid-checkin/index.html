What's working: landing page clicks got cheaper. In Wave 1, cost per LP click fell from $12 (Oct 3 to 6) to $9 over Oct 7 to 8, and CPC fell from $4 to $2. Wave 2 launched straight at $2.50 and got LP clicks at $7 on its first day, against $13 for the Oct 6 launches at $4.

What isn't: delivery fell by more than a third. Wave 1 impressions per campaign per day went from 28 to 18 over Oct 7 to 8 (down 35%) and to 16 on Oct 8. Comparing like for like, the 77 campaigns live since Oct 2 went from $248 to $60 spend per day and from 21 to 7 LP clicks per day. On Oct 8, 127 of 134 Wave 1 campaigns spent under 30% of budget. Wave 2 also delivered less on its first day than the Oct 6 launches did at $4 (15 vs 36 impressions per campaign). The bid cut bought cheaper clicks, but fewer of them.

Does the 8am to 8pm schedule change this? Not the conclusion. The schedule is the same before and after the change, so the before vs after comparison holds. A $10 budget is reachable inside the window: Scan Global Logistics spent $12 a day at a $2 bid, and many $4 campaigns spent $12 a day before the change. So pacing is still measured against $10. If you'd rather use half the budget as the target ($5, since the group is live about half the day), the "under 30%" cut drops to $1.50 a day and the raise list would shrink to 25. The schedule does make Oct 7 a cleaner "after" day: the bid change landed at 8:24am ET, about 24 minutes into the window, so Oct 7 was almost all at $2.50.
