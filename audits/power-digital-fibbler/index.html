Things to Know

  • Influence. A deal counts when the company reached the selected threshold of paid LinkedIn activity before the deal: 3+ paid clicks, 3+ paid engagements, or 100+ paid impressions. Every number on the page follows the filters at the top.
  • Segment. Read from the campaign name: CPG (PDM_CPG and Food & Bev), Lifestyle (PDM_LIF), Fashion (PDM_FSH and Fashion), B2B (PDM_B2B), and Retargeting (any campaign with Retargeting in the name). A deal touched by campaigns in more than one segment shows under each one, so segments can overlap. All includes older 2025 campaigns that sit outside these segments.
  • Create vs close. Pipeline, the deal table, campaign-by-deal cards and journeys use the create date. Influenced revenue uses the close date (Closed Won only). A deal created last year and won this year counts as revenue in the period it closed.
  • Pipeline. Pipeline created = all deals created in the period, any stage. Open pipeline = deals not yet Closed Won, Closed Lost or Disqualified.
  • Deal amounts. Many deals reach Fibbler from HubSpot without an amount. They still count as influenced deals but add $0 to pipeline and revenue, so dollar totals are a floor.
  • Campaign credit overlaps. A deal counts in full under every campaign that reached it, so campaign pipeline and revenue can double count and should not be added up across campaigns.
  • Currency. Ad spend and deal amounts are both USD. No ROAS is shown, because influenced revenue counts each deal at its full value.
  • Time frames. YTD = Jan 1 to Nov 9, 2026. L3M = Aug to Oct 2026. LFM = October 2026. MTD = Nov 1 to Nov 9, 2026. November is partial. Recent months can fill in as HubSpot deals sync to Fibbler.
  • Ad type mix. Spotlight ads are excluded from the mix.
  • Source. Fibbler (LinkedIn Ads plus HubSpot), data as of Nov 9, 2026 ET. Campaign links open LinkedIn Campaign Manager.
