Customer journeys0

Open and Closed Won deals that match the filters, sorted by amount. Each card is the 12 calendar months through the month the deal was created. A month shows paid impressions, clicks, and engagements when Fibbler reported them. n/a means that month was not reported, not that it was zero. Things to Know

  • Possible job titles. Estimated by weighting each campaign’s LinkedIn title impression shares by that company’s Fibbler campaign impressions. Title reports come from Fibbler (top 25 titles per campaign) and cover January 2025 through October 2026; they are not limited to months before each opportunity’s create month. Coverage is the share of company campaign impressions with matching title data. Rankings describe possible roles, with approximate and suppressed LinkedIn demographic counts. Rankings stay tied to the opportunity when the report’s period filter changes. Data pulled November 9, 2026.
  • Influence. A deal counts when paid LinkedIn activity before the deal reaches the selected threshold: 3+ paid clicks, 3+ paid engagements, or 100+ paid impressions. Every deal number on the page uses the same filters.
  • Business type. Most Power Digital deals have no deal type in HubSpot, so the page does not split new and existing business. Every influenced deal is included and listed once.
  • Create vs close. Pipeline, the deal table, campaign-by-deal cards, and journeys use the create date. Influenced revenue uses the close date (Closed Won only). A deal created last year and won this year counts as revenue in the period it closed.
  • Pipeline. Pipeline created includes every filtered deal created in the period, any stage. Open pipeline is deals that are not Closed Won, Closed Lost or Disqualified. Disqualified is treated like Closed Lost.
  • Cost per opp created. Each filtered opportunity created in the period is counted as a demo proxy, including deals with no amount. That equivalence is an assumption, not a verified demo event. The figure is LinkedIn spend for all campaigns divided by that count. It is not paid causal attribution. A dash means no opportunities were created in the view.
  • Currency. Spend and deal amounts are USD. Money is rounded to the nearest dollar.
  • Time frames. YTD is Jan 1 to Nov 14, 2026. L3M is Aug 1 to Oct 31, 2026. LFM is October 2026. MTD is Nov 1 to Nov 14, 2026. November covers 14 of 30 days.
  • Coverage. Nov 14, 2026 snapshot for Fibbler Power Digital Marketing (cm3vwfcg400sd8qqtzb4amxvl) and LinkedIn ads account 509859857. Spend covers all 83 LinkedIn campaigns that delivered in 2026 and sums to $730,406 for Jan through Nov 14. This file has 373 influenced deals, matching Fibbler’s count for Jan through Nov. 229 deals have no amount in HubSpot and count as $0. 111 deals have no campaign split. Journeys are pulled per company from Fibbler.
  • Ad type mix. Includes every LinkedIn format that spent in the period, Spotlight included. Campaign groups come from the campaign name prefix (CPG, Lifestyle, Fashion, B2B, Retargeting).
