    Bid vs DeliveryiActive ad sets only (status Active or on audience hold, as of Oct 31, 2026). x = current bid, y = spend in the last 7 days (Oct 24 to 30), color = targeting type. Dots on the red $0 line did not deliver. Always last 7 days; does not follow the tabs. Hover for the company.

    Account EfficiencyiOne dot per company for the selected time frame (companies with spend). x = spend, y = LP clicks, bubble size = impressions. Dashed line = the overall average Cost/LP for the period. Dots below the line pay more than average per LP click (red); dots far below it are wasted spend. Green = at or better than average.

    Targeting ComparisoniCost/LP (left bar, left axis) and LP CTR (right bar, right axis) for each targeting type in the selected time frame. Label above each pair = accounts with impressions or spend. Green = best of the three, red = worst.

    Needs AttentioniActive 1 to 1 ad sets only, ad set status as of Oct 31, 2026 (campaign groups paused overnight for dayparting are not excluded). No spend 7+ days = Active, not on audience hold, $0 spend Oct 24 to 30. High Cost/LP = at least $100 spend in the last 30 days and Cost/LP at least 2x the 1 to 1 average (or 2x the average spent with 0 LP clicks). Click a card or an issue chip to filter.

    By Campaign ObjectiveiAccounts = distinct companies with impressions or spend in the period.

    TargetingiMG = "MG" in the ad set or campaign name. Marketing only = "[Mkt Only]" in the name, or Seniority/Function targeting whose only job function is Marketing. Rows do not overlap and add up to the total. Account counts can overlap across rows because one company can have several ad sets.

    Bottom 50 Spending Ad SetsiLowest 50 by spend after the filters, including $0 ad sets that are Active now or delivered in the period. Default sort: spend low to high, then impressions and LP clicks high to low. Status filter defaults to Active. Current bid as of Oct 31, 2026.

    Ad PerformanceiV1, V2, V3 (new) = new 1 to 1 template ads (from Sep 29, 2026). V10 Billboard = the purple city billboard creative. Preview = top spending ad of that version over the last 60 days; click to enlarge.

    Creative FatigueiCTR (all clicks / impressions) in the last 14 days (Oct 17 to 30, 2026) vs the first 14 days the version or ad delivered. Flagged when CTR dropped 30%+. Versions need 1,000+ impressions in both windows; single ads need 200+. Does not follow the time frame tabs.
