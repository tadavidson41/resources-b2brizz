1 to 1 ABM LinkedIn Ads Performance

1 to 1 ABM ad sets only. Data through yesterday, pulled Oct 31, 2026.

Overview

Daily Trend

Spend and LP clicks per day for the selected time frame.

Fibbler Influence

1 to 1 accounts only. Deals created or closed in the last 12 months (Fibbler 365d, LinkedIn source) where the company had 3+ paid clicks. Open and won only. New Business combined per company; Upsell and Cross Sell kept as separate deals; Renewals excluded. Does not follow the time frame tabs.

Partnership split

Needs Attention

Active 1 to 1 ad sets only, as of Oct 30, 2026. High Cost/LP = at least $100 spend in the last 30 days and Cost/LP at least 2x the 1 to 1 average (or 2x the average spent with 0 LP clicks). Click a header to sort.

By Campaign Objective

Accounts = distinct companies with impressions or spend in the period.

Targeting

MG = "MG" in the ad set or campaign name. Marketing only = "[Mkt Only]" in the name, or Seniority/Function targeting whose only job function is Marketing.

Top 50 Spending Ad Sets

Click any column header to sort. Status is the ad set's own status, not the campaign group's. "Audience too small" overrides Active or Paused.

Bottom 50 Spending Ad Sets

Includes ad sets with $0 spend that are Active now or delivered in the period. Default sort: spend low to high, then impressions and LP clicks high to low. Current bid as of Oct 31, 2026. Click any column header to sort.

Ad Performance

"V1 (new)" etc. = new 1 to 1 template ads (from Sep 29, 2026). "Old V#" = the earlier 2025 to early 2026 1 to 1 series. Preview = top spending ad of that version over the last 60 days; click to enlarge. Green = top three LP CTR and three lowest Cost/LP in each table.

Creative Fatigue

CTR (all clicks / impressions) in the last 14 days (Oct 17 to 30, 2026) vs the first 14 days the version or ad delivered. Flagged when CTR dropped 30%+. Versions need 1,000+ impressions in both windows; single ads need 200+. Fatigue badges above use the version check. Does not follow the time frame tabs.
